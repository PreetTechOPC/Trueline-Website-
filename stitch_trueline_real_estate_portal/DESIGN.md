## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an uncompromising standard of ultra-luxury, architectural discipline, and quiet institutional authority tailored for sovereign private wealth and trophy real estate acquisitions. The aesthetic is restrained, immaculate, and deliberately uncluttered—drawing inspiration from contemporary bespoke architecture, private sovereign portfolios, and elite editorial publications.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Architectural Rigor:** Clean geometric tension, razor-sharp alignment, and structural symmetry that echo landmark physical structures.
- **Editorial Restraint:** Excessive ornamentation is rejected in favor of vast negative space, high-precision typography, and luminous, razor-thin metallic touches.
- **Submerged Radiance:** Surfaces are bathed in deep midnight voids, punctuated strictly by warm metallic luminescence rather than saturated glows.
- **Sensory Authority:** Every micro-interaction is measured, weighted, and deliberate—eschewing playful physics for steady, cinematic transitions.

## Colors

The palette derives its gravity from midnight oceanic depths and crystalline architectural steel, elevated by sovereign gold accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Core (`#D8AA36`):** Luminous Warm Gold used for primary calls-to-action, key active markers, and architectural keyline accents.
- **Secondary Highlight (`#F0C450`):** Light Gold reserved for hover states, focus indicators, and subtle illuminated gradients.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#012498`):** Royal Indigo applied sparingly to structural grounding elements, delicate backdrop tinting, and sovereign credentialing badges.
- **Base Abyss (`#040e2b`):** Deep Navy/Midnight canvas providing an impenetrable, luxurious background layer.
- **Structural Elevated Surface (`#071C51`):** Deep Architectural Midnight used for floating card planes, drawer sheets, and table headers.

### Surface Tones & Typography Legibility
- **Primary Typography & High Accent:** Off-White (`#F8F8F6`) delivered with unyielding contrast against dark voids.
- **Subdued Editorial Body:** Architectural Muted Grey (`#B7C0C9`) ensuring fatigue-free narrative reading.
- **Borders & Framing:** 10%–18% alpha tints of Warm Gold (`rgba(216, 170, 54, 0.16)`) and Midnight Indigo (`rgba(1, 36, 152, 0.3)`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate dialogue between classical monumentality and sleek technical precision.

- **Headlines (Montserrat):** Set with deliberate weight and slight tracking adjustments. `label-caps` is deployed strictly in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.18em`) for metric callouts, asset codes, architectural square footages, and section eyebrow markers.
- **Body & Editorial Narratives (Inter):** Rendered primarily in lighter weights (`300` to `400`) to guarantee high-end breathability. Generous line-heights mimic print editorial books.
- **Numerals & Metrics:** Large currency and structural dimensions leverage Montserrat Medium with tight kerning for maximum architectural gravitas.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column dynamic grid framed by substantial outward breathing room, reminiscent of master blueprints and luxury art monographs.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns, `margin-desktop` (5rem/80px), `gutter-desktop` (3rem/48px). Maximum container width is pinned to `1600px` for ultra-wide monitors to maintain composed readability.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin-tablet` (3rem/48px), `gutter-tablet` (2rem/32px).
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1.25rem/20px), `gutter-mobile` (1rem/16px).

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm favors massive expansion over compression. Feature components and portfolio imagery utilize `4xl` (6rem) to `5xl` (8rem) margins to convey scarcity and grandeur.
- Nested UI clusters adhere to strict 8pt proportional scales (`xs` through `xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the sleek, modern aesthetic, physical drop-shadows are strictly minimized in favor of tonal layered surfaces and low-contrast metallic keylines.

### Surface Tiers
1. **The Canvas (Level 0):** `#040e2b` solid baseline.
2. **Elevated Monoliths (Level 1):** `#071C51` with a hairline top and left border (`1px solid rgba(216, 170, 54, 0.18)`), creating a chiseled stone illusion.
3. **Modal & Overlays (Level 2):** Backdrop blur of `24px` mixed with 85% opacity `#040e2b`, bordered completely with `1px solid rgba(240, 196, 80, 0.25)`.

### Ambient Glows
Diffused illumination is only used to denote active interaction. Ambient glow values must not exceed `0 20px 50px rgba(1, 36, 152, 0.35)` to sustain architectural poise without drifting into neon tropes.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly **Sharp (`roundedness`: 0)**.

- All buttons, structural cards, image wrappers, and interactive panels have pure `0px` border radii.
- This clean, orthogonal geometric discipline reinforces modern architectural facets, steel I-beams, floor-to-ceiling plate glass, and the absolute permanence of landmark real estate.
- The only rounded forms permitted within the system are circular status indicators and avatar cutouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign:** Solid `#D8AA36` fill, text in `#040e2b` (Montserrat SemiBold, `label-caps`), zero border-radius. On hover, shifts to `#F0C450` with an expansive horizontal letter-spacing animation (+0.04em).
- **Secondary Frame:** Transparent fill, `1px solid rgba(216, 170, 54, 0.4)` border, text in `#F8F8F6`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(216, 170, 54, 0.08)` and border brightens to `#F0C450`.
- **Text Action:** Architectural underline offset by `8px` with a persistent `1px` rule in `#D8AA36`, animating from left to right on interaction.

### Real Estate Listing & Asset Cards
- Minimalist framed containers featuring an elevated surface `#071C51` masked inside a 1px border of `rgba(216, 170, 54, 0.12)`.
- Image frames utilize a cinematic 16:10 or 3:2 ratio with an ultra-subtle slow zoom (scale 1.03 over 800ms ease-out) on hover.
- Price and location information are grouped cleanly using Montserrat for currency values and `label-caps` in `#B7C0C9` for geographical coordinates or district codes.

### Input Fields & Search Filters
- Understated architectural inputs: Solid transparent backgrounds accented strictly by an active bottom-rule or an ultra-fine border `1px solid rgba(183, 192, 201, 0.25)`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#D8AA36` with zero glow; placeholder text shifts upward into an uppercase micro-label (`label-caps`) in `#D8AA36`.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp, rectangular asset tags (`0px` radius).
- Background: `rgba(1, 36, 152, 0.4)` with an integrated hairline border in `rgba(216, 170, 54, 0.3)`. Text rendered in `#F8F8F6` using `label-caps`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square (`0px` radius) toggles framed in `1px solid rgba(216, 170, 54, 0.5)`.
- Checked state: Interior filled with a centered, solid `#D8AA36` geometric square inset by 3px. No soft checkmarks; pure geometric indicators.

### Curated Lists & Asset Specifications
- Specification tables (e.g., Gross Internal Area, Ceiling Height, Terrestrial Bounds) feature horizontal border separators set to `1px solid rgba(183, 192, 201, 0.12)`.
- Metric keys rendered in `#B7C0C9` (`body-sm`), metric values rendered in `#F8F8F6` (Montserrat Medium).
